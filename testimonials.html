COMMON INQUIRIES

Client Experience & Guarantees

Transparent answers to what you can expect when partnering with NEO Kitchens.

How does NEO Kitchens maintain its 98.4% on-time delivery rate?
We employ a dedicated in-house project manager for each residence and pre-order all long-lead materials (such as custom European cabinetry, slabs, and luxury appliances) before a single hammer swings. This guarantees zero downtime waiting on shipments once active demolition begins.
Can I speak directly with past clients in my metropolitan area?
Absolutely. Many of our satisfied homeowners participate in our Ambassador Circle and gladly invite serious prospective clients for private walk-throughs or telephone reference consultations. Simply let your design advisor know during your discovery session.
What guarantees and post-completion warranties do you provide?
Every custom kitchen and renovation is backed by our comprehensive 5-Year Craftsmanship Guarantee, along with manufacturer lifetime warranties on drawer glides, hinges, and premium cabinet finishes. We also conduct a complimentary 6-month and 12-month post-handover inspection to fine-tune alignments.
